The problem

A fake looks exactly like the real thing.

Fraudsters copy a bank's wording, its sender name and its tone. Often the only difference is a single character.

Your bankwww.demobank.ng/app
vs
A fraudsterwww.demo-bank.ng/app

Customers can't be expected to spot that. The bank's own records can, once every message is registered with Gaskiyya.

How customers check

One question. A straight answer.

Inside the bank's own app through the Gaskiyya API, or in the Gaskiyya app. Either way, the answer comes from the bank's records.

  1. 01

    Paste it, share it, or snap it

    Copy a suspicious SMS or WhatsApp message, share it straight from your messages app, paste a link, or send a screenshot.

  2. 02

    Fingerprinted on your phone

    Gaskiyya turns the message into a fingerprint on your phone. Only the fingerprint decides the result, never your words. To explain a result that is not confirmed, the text is sent once with long numbers hidden.

  3. 03

    Your bank's records answer

    If your bank sent that exact message to your number, you'll see Sent by your bank, with the channel, date and time.

  4. 04

    Not on record? You'll know why

    You'll see what your bank really sent you recently, and plain-language reasons the message looks like a scam.

Privacy

          
Built so there's nothing to leak.

          

            
Your words stay on your phone
Messages are fingerprinted on your device. The text is never sent or stored.

            
No phone numbers stored
Records hold scrambled keys only. Even a breach reveals no numbers and no messages.

            
Only your bank confirms
An answer of "sent by your bank" comes from an exact match in your bank's records, never from a guess or an AI.

            
When in doubt, it says so
If a check can't be completed, you're told plainly. Gaskiyya never guesses "genuine".

FAQ

          
Frequently asked questions

          
Unsure about a message right now? Don't reply or tap anything. Contact your bank through its app or a branch.

        

        

        
Does Gaskiyya read my SMS inbox?
No. You choose what to check by pasting, sharing or taking a screenshot. The app never reads your messages on its own.

        
Why might a real message show as "not confirmed"?
Only messages your bank has registered with Gaskiyya can be confirmed. Banks enrol one message type at a time, so treat "not confirmed" as a reason to be careful, and contact your bank through its app or a branch.

        
Can a fraudster make a fake message pass?
Not by copying it. A confirmation needs an exact match with a record your bank created for your number. Change one character, or send it to someone else, and it won't match.

        
Does AI decide whether a message is genuine?
Never. The answer comes only from your bank's records. AI is used afterwards, only to explain in plain words why something looks risky.

        
Will Gaskiyya ever ask for my PIN or a one-time code?
Never. If anyone claiming to be Gaskiyya or your bank asks for your PIN, password, card number or a one-time code, it's a scam.

        
Is Gaskiyya an app or a service for banks?
Both, in that order. Gaskiyya is verification infrastructure that banks connect to their messaging and contact centre. Customers then check messages and calls in their bank's own app, or in the Gaskiyya app.

        
Does it work with my bank?
Only if your bank uses Gaskiyya. A message can be confirmed only when the bank registered it, so the app links your number through your bank's own login. If your bank is not on Gaskiyya yet, the app cannot confirm its messages; ask your bank to join.
